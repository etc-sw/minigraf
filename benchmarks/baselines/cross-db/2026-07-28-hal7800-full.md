The 8.513 ms append p95 that the previous baseline flagged as uncomfortably
close to Vetch's 10 ms cadence budget is now 2.558 ms. That headroom warning is
retired for this profile.

The RSS rows are not measured the same way and must not be subtracted. The old
number was one process-wide high-water mark across build, checkpoint, reopen,
reads, and scan. This run measures the scan in a fresh isolated process, so
515.949 MiB is scan-attributable. The whole-run stress process peaked at
510.410 MiB.

## Development judgment

- Every engine preserved exact data and recovered its acknowledged prefix after
  kill-9. Stability is still not the comparative weakness.
- Against the closest semantic peer, Vicia now leads build, append, point read,
  and engine aggregate, and trails on reopen, materialized scan, and storage.
- **Reopen (11.254 ms vs Cozo's 0.291 ms) is the largest remaining gap** — 39×,
  and it is paid on every open, unlike the scan. It barely moved since 2026-07-12
  while everything around it improved by 40–70%.
- Materialized scan is 2.03× Cozo and 43× SQLite. It is also the column a
  default-configured Vicia handle refuses outright at this size: the scan crosses
  `DEFAULT_MAX_RESULTS`, and the harness raises the bound to measure past it. The
  engine aggregate column — 174.206 ms, 1.88× faster than Cozo, at 26.727 MiB — is
  the shape ordinary callers actually use.
- Storage is 3.4× Cozo and 22.9× SQLite per fact. Vicia stores bi-temporal
  identity plus four graph indexes; Cozo's fixture is a current EAV relation,
  SQLite is a hand-shaped current table, and redb removes query, graph, and
  temporal semantics entirely. Their smaller byte counts are cost floors, not
  evidence of equivalent behavior.

Do not compare these values with another host unless the exact profile, source,
durability settings, and host provenance match.
